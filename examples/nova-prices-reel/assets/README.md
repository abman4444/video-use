# Assets that are not part of the composition

Files here are **outside `public/`** on purpose: Remotion bundles everything under
`public/` into every render, and nothing in this folder is loaded by the composition.

## `am-logo-sting-3d.mp4`

A 1920×1080 logo animation — the `AM` letters tumbling in and assembling into the
house-and-key mark on a white studio ground.

It is deliberately **not** in the reel. At the 144px the closing lockup allows, the
render's keyed edges go soft and a flat mark is visibly crisper; a logo sting wants to be
full-frame. Use it as a top or tail card in the edit, around this composition rather than
inside it.

If a dimensional mark is ever wanted at a larger size, grab a frame of the assembled logo
and key the background to transparent.
